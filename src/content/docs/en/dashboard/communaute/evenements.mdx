<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Events</strong> module creates three kinds of activity: <strong>quizzes</strong>, <strong>CTFs</strong> (security challenges) and <strong>custom events</strong> with sign-ups.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Moderation → Events** section: [Open events ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/events).
Kotbo needs the **Manage events** permission to create a scheduled Discord event.
:::

Three indicators open the page: **Total Events**, **Ongoing** and **Participations / Registrations**.

---

## Choosing a type

| Type | Tag | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quiz** | Questions & Answers | Multiple-choice or free-text questions, to test your community's knowledge. |
| **CTF** | Hacking & Security | Computer-security challenges with hidden flags, a real-time leaderboard and roles as rewards. |
| **Custom** | Registration & Announcement | A Discord event, a polished announcement and sign-ups by form or by button. |

An event moves through the **Draft**, **Published**, **Ongoing**, **Completed** and **Cancelled** statuses.

---

## Configuring an event

The **General Configuration** is shared by all three types:

* **Event title** and **Description**.
* **Announcement Channel** (or *None* to announce nothing).
* **Registration Form**: a form from the [Forms](/en/dashboard/communaute/formulaires/) module, or *None* for direct button sign-up.
* **Start date** and **End date** (optional), in local time.
* **Location**: Discord, in person, and so on.
* **Create the event on Discord**: adds it to the server's scheduled events.
* **Send an announcement embed**: posts a message with a sign-up button.

### Automatic trigger

An event can launch by itself:

* **Manual**: no trigger.
* **Member count reached**: set the **target member count**.
* **Scheduled**: set the **launch date and time**.

The **Trigger Status** then reports *Pending*, *Triggered* or *Failed*.

## Quiz editor

The **Add a question** button creates an entry with its **question text** and its **answer options**. Tick the circle to the left of the correct answer.

The **Import CSV** button loads a batch of questions. The file must contain the `Question`, `Option X` and `Bonne Réponse` columns.

---

## CTF editor

The **Add a challenge** button creates an entry with:

* **Challenge title** and **Description / Brief**;
* **Expected flag** and **Points**;
* **XP Reward** and **Reward role** (both optional);
* **Image URL** (optional).

---

## Steering an event live

The **Control** button opens the control room.

### For a quiz

* **Next Question** and **Previous** move the quiz forward or back.
* Progress is shown as *Question X / Y*.
* The **Chart** tab shows answers arriving live.
* The **Participants** tab ranks players by **Score** and shows their **last answer**.
* **Finish Quiz** closes the event.

### For a CTF

The **Challenges** tab counts solves per challenge and lists the **latest solves**. The **Participants** tab ranks players by score and by last solve.

### For a custom event

The **Registrations** tab lists sign-ups with their **registration date** and the answers to the linked form. The **Remove** button cancels a registration.

---

## Deleting

:::danger[Irreversible deletion]
Deleting an event erases every question, answer and participant attached to it.
:::
