<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Giveaways</strong> module posts a giveaway message on Discord with a "Join" button. Kotbo counts entries, picks winners, and lets you reroll if needed.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Community → Giveaways** section: [Open giveaways ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/giveaways).
Administrators and members with **Manage messages** have access by default.
:::

---

## Giveaways tab

### Starting a giveaway

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Click Start a Giveaway</h3><p>The <strong>Start a Giveaway</strong> form opens.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Describe the prize</h3><p><strong>Prize / Reward</strong> is required; the <strong>Description</strong> carries conditions or extra details.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Set up the draw</h3><p><strong>Number of Winners</strong> and <strong>Giveaway Duration</strong>, in minutes, hours or days. Preset durations are offered.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Pick the channel</h3><p>Then click <strong>Send to Discord</strong>.</p></div></div>
</div>

### Following and steering

Every giveaway in the list shows its status (<span class="dash-badge active">Active</span> or <span class="dash-badge">Ended</span>), its participant count, its number of winners and its end date.

| Button | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Pick Winner** | Forces the giveaway to end and draws immediately. |
| **Reroll** | Runs a new draw. |
| **Delete from dashboard** | Removes the giveaway from the dashboard. |

Drawn winners appear under the giveaway. When staff validation is required, they are listed under **Winner(s) awaiting validation**. Clicking a name opens the member's file.

## Configuration tab

### Manager roles

Defines who can start, end, reroll and delete a giveaway. These roles add to administrators and to members with **Manage messages**, and they gain the `/giveaway` command on Discord.

### Participation

* **Required roles**: leave empty to open giveaways to the whole server.
* **Excluded roles**: these take priority over required roles. An excluded member can never take part.

Remember to click **Save configuration**.

---

## Public page

A public link shares the list of running giveaways with no sign-in and no dashboard access. Visitors see **Active** and **Ended** giveaways, the time remaining, the number of entries and the winners drawn.

Entering, however, always happens from the Discord message using the "Join" button.

:::tip[Bonus rewards]
A giveaway can also hand out coins, RPG XP or an item from the [Economy & RPG](/en/dashboard/economie/economie/) module. Those show on the public page under **Bonus rewards**.
:::
