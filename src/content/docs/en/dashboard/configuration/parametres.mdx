<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>General settings</strong> gather what several modules use in common: the system channels, the base staff roles and the main integrations.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Configuration → Settings** section: [Open the settings ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/settings).
Access requires the permission to manage server settings.
:::

---

## Discord channels

These channels act as the default for modules that do not declare one of their own:

| Setting | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Public channel** | The general public channel. |
| **Logs channel** | The activity log channel. |
| **Config channel** | The internal configuration channel. |
| **News channel** | Where patch notes and staff news are published. |
| **Daily Algo channel** | Where the daily exercises are published. |
| **Meeting voice** | The default voice channel for meetings. |

---

## Staff roles

* **Base Staff Role**: the team's base role.
* **Trial Staff Role**: the role for members in their trial period.

These two roles are what the [Staff directory](/en/dashboard/staff/annuaire/) and [Tutoring](/en/dashboard/staff/tutorat/) rely on.

---

## Integrations

Quick switches for the cross-cutting modules:

* **Auto-translation**: translation of content.
* **Code Police**: audit of code posted on the server.
* **Daily Algo**: daily challenges.
* **GitHub Releases**: new version notifications.
* **Propagate sanctions**: cross-server synchronisation.
* **Cross-server record**: share and view sanctions from other servers on the instance.

---

## Activity statistics

The **Activity statistics** switch measures member activity: messages, voice, reactions.

:::caution[What switching it off stops]
Turned off, nothing is recorded for this server any more: messages, voice minutes, reactions, threads, joins and leaves, per-member statistics, word frequency and per-module usage rankings.

The other modules (moderation, tickets, levels and so on) keep working normally. Only the bot's technical measurements (execution times and errors, with no identity at all) are kept.
:::

This is the server's main privacy lever. Switched off, the [Analytics](/en/dashboard/general/analytics/) page and [Pulse](/en/dashboard/general/pulse/) have nothing left to show.

---

## Saving

Changes go through the save bar at the bottom of the screen. Kotbo confirms with *Global settings saved*.

A per-field history shows who changed what, and everything is also recorded in the [Activity log](/en/dashboard/moderation/journal-activite/).
