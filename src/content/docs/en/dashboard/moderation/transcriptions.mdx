<p class="doc-lead">A <strong>transcript</strong> is a readable copy of a channel's contents, generated by Kotbo when the channel is closed. It is what remains of a support ticket once its channel is gone.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Moderation → Transcripts** section: [Open transcripts ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/transcripts-list).
The page belongs to the **Support tickets** module: it disappears if that module is turned off.
:::

---

## Where transcripts come from

Kotbo generates a transcript in two situations:

* when a **ticket channel is deleted**, in the [Support tickets](/en/dashboard/communaute/tickets/) module. The transcript is mandatory there: it is produced before the channel disappears, and its link is posted in the log channel. Closing a ticket, on the other hand, generates none, since the channel still exists;
* on a **moderator's request**, when they import Discord messages as evidence into a [sanction report](/en/dashboard/moderation/sanctions/). A separate transcript is then created for each channel kept.

---

## Consulting

* The counter at the top shows how many transcripts are stored.
* The search field filters by **channel name**.
* The **Open** button displays the full transcript: messages, authors, timestamps and attachments.
* **Load more** fetches the next entries.

If the list is empty, Kotbo shows either *No results for this search* or *Generated transcripts will appear here*.

---

## Deleting

The delete icon asks for confirmation right on the row (**Confirm?**) before erasing the transcript.

:::danger[Permanent deletion]
A deleted transcript cannot be recovered. If it backs a sanction report, that report loses its evidence.
:::
