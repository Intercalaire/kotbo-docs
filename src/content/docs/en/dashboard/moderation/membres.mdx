<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Members</strong> page is your server directory. Use it to find a member, review their activity and open their moderation file.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Moderation → Members** section: [Open the directory ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/members).
Access requires a moderation or administration role.
:::

---

## The indicators at the top

Four figures summarise the state of your community:

* **Total members**
* the number of members **online**;
* the number of members who **left**;
* the number of **bots**.

---

## Finding a member

The search field accepts a **name**, a **server nickname** or a **Discord ID**.

### Filtering

* **Humans** / **Bots**: restricts the list to one or the other.
* **On server** / **Left**: Kotbo keeps the record of members who left, so you can still look up the history of a departed account.
* **Reset the filters** clears everything.

### Sorting

Three sorts, each available ascending or descending:

* **Last activity**
* **Messages sent**
* **Join date**

The **Per page** selector controls how many rows are shown, and the counter at the bottom tells you how many members are visible out of the total.

---

## Reading a row

Each row shows the member, their **Activity** column (last activity, in minutes, hours or days, or *Never*) and their **Messages** column.

The **File** button opens the member's full record: sanction history, roles, join date, activity, and the moderation actions you can apply straight from the dashboard.

:::tip[From other pages]
A member's file is also reachable by clicking their name on the [Sanctions](/en/dashboard/moderation/sanctions/), [Discord logs](/en/dashboard/moderation/logs/) and [Alt accounts](/en/dashboard/moderation/double-comptes/) pages.
:::
