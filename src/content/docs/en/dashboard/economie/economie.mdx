<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Economy & RPG</strong> module adds a local currency, an item shop, random text adventures and player guilds.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Economy & RPG → Economy & RPG** section: [Open the economy ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/economy).
The module is off by default: turn it on with the **Module status** switch at the top of the page.
:::

---

## Picking a pace

A pace configures the daily reward, its cooldown and energy in one go.

| Pace | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Steady** | Modest gains and one daily a day: the currency keeps its value over time. |
| **Standard** *(recommended)* | A daily slightly more frequent than once a day, regular adventures. |
| **Generous** | Lots of coins and back-to-back adventures, for a server that is starting out. |
| **Custom** | Your own values, tuned one by one. |

The tiles show the **daily reward**, its **cooldown**, the total **per day** and the **energy**. A pace never changes the currency name, its emoji, or the RPG, shop and guild modules.

## Configuration tab

### Module activation

* **Enable RPG Adventures (RNG)**: unlocks the `/rpg-travel` command and random events.
* **Enable Item Shop**: allows buying gear, equipping weapons and using potions.
* **Enable RPG Guild System**: allows founding alliances, cooperating and holding a guild vault.

### Settings

* **Currency name**, **Currency emoji** and **Currency image** (PNG, JPG or SVG; the image takes priority on the dashboard).
* **Minimum** and **Maximum Daily Reward**, **Daily Cooldown (Hours)**.
* **Adventure Cooldown (Min)**.
* **Maximum Energy** and **Energy Recovery / Hour**.

### Anti-abuse limits

Caps applied to gambling games and member-to-member transfers:

* **Maximum bet per game**: applies to `/dice`, `/roulette` and `/rps`.
* **Games per day and per member**.
* **Maximum amount per transfer**: applies to `/rpg-pay`.
* **Delay between two transfers (minutes)**.

### Reset

Five buttons, all irreversible and confirmed by a dedicated dialog: **Players** (accounts, inventories, balances), **Shop** (created items), **Guilds**, **Configuration** (multipliers and cooldowns) and **Global Reset**.

## Shop Items tab

The **Create item** button opens a form:

* **Item name**, **Emoji**, **Description**, **Item type** and **Purchase price**.
* **Statistics & Effects**: **Attack Bonus (ATK)**, **Defense Bonus (DEF)**, **Health Restore (HP)** and **Energy Restore**. Not every type needs attributes.

Items provided globally by Kotbo appear as **read-only** and cannot be edited from your server.

---

## Players & Leaderboard tab

The table ranks players with the **Rank**, **Player**, **Balance**, **Stats & Gear**, **HP & Energy** and **Location & Guild** columns. A search field accepts a nickname or an ID.

The **Edit** button opens the player's profile, where you can adjust their **balance**, **RPG level**, **experience**, **health points**, **energy**, **attack (ATK)**, **defense (DEF)** and **speed (SPD)**.

---

## Black market tab

A clandestine shop that opens at unpredictable dates and times. Each member sees their **own selection** of shop items, heavily discounted and in limited quantity.

:::caution[The RPG shop is required]
Offers are drawn from the purchasable items. With no active shop, the black market has nothing to offer.
:::

* **Opening rhythm**: **Period (days)**, the average gap between two openings, the exact moment staying random, and **Opening duration (minutes)**.
* **Drawn offers**: **Offers per member**, **Max purchases per offer**, **Minimum discount (%)** and **Maximum discount (%)**.
* **Announcement**: *None, discovered through `/rpg`*, *Message in a channel*, or *Message + role mention*.
