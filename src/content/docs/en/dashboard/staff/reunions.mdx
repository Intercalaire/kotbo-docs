<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Meetings</strong> module schedules team check-ins, announces them on Discord with RSVP buttons, and records attendance automatically.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Staff → Meetings** section: [Open meetings ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/meetings).
:::

---

## Scheduling a meeting

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Click New Meeting</h3><p>The <strong>Schedule a Meeting</strong> form opens.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Fill in the title and dates</h3><p><strong>Meeting title</strong> and <strong>Planned start</strong> are required. The <strong>Planned end</strong> must come after the start.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Write the agenda</h3><p>The <strong>Agenda / Description</strong> field is published with the announcement.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Schedule it</h3><p>Kotbo announces the event in the dedicated Discord channel and enables the RSVP buttons.</p></div></div>
</div>

:::note[The time zone of the dates you enter]
Below the date fields, Kotbo recalls the zone the time is read in: *Time interpreted in …*. By default that is the **server's time zone**, set from the [dashboard home](/en/dashboard/general/accueil/).

The **Change** button lets you enter the time in another zone, for this meeting only; **Use the server's time zone** returns to the shared setting. Changing zone **reinterprets** the displayed time, it does not rewrite it.
:::

A meeting whose date has already passed is refused, and the form offers an upcoming slot from the start.

## Configuring the channels

The **Meeting Channels** block defines:

* the **announcement channel** where the event is published;
* the **voice channel** where the meeting takes place.

The lists separate channels on **this server** from those on the linked **staff server**, when there is one.

The **Role Access** block controls which roles can see and join meetings.

---

## Following along

A meeting moves through the **Scheduled**, **In progress**, **Completed** and **Cancelled** statuses.

* **Start** moves the meeting into progress.
* **Finish** closes it.
* **Details** opens the full record, with the **attendance list**.

### Attendance

Three counters summarise turnout: **Present**, **Excused** and **Absent**. An absence declared in advance shows as **Justified absence**, attendance being recorded automatically in the voice channel.

:::tip[Cross-check with absences]
An absence declared in the [Planning](/en/dashboard/staff/planning/) over a meeting slot automatically justifies the member: no need to mark them excused by hand.
:::

## Deleting a meeting

Deletion is irreversible in the database. Kotbo then asks what to clean up on Discord:

* **Delete the Discord event**: removes it from the server calendar.
* **Delete the announcement message**: erases the message and its buttons.
* **Delete the internal notifications**: cleans the dashboard Inbox for every member.
