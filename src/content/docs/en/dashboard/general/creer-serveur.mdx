<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Build my server</strong> page lays down a server's entire structure in a single operation: categories, channels, roles and permissions, already wired to the Kotbo modules.</p>

:::danger[Once per server only]
The setup can be run **only once**. Once executed, the page shows who ran it and when. Contact support if it has to be run again.
:::

:::note[Where to find it]
The **General → Build my server** section: [Open the assistant ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/server-template). The page is in **beta**.
:::

---

## Checking the permissions

Kotbo tells you at the top of the page whether it has the permissions it needs. If any are missing, it lists them and reminds you that **the simplest option is to give it the Administrator role for the duration of the setup**.

Channels will be named in the server's language.

---

## Choosing what gets created

The **What Kotbo will create** panel lists everything that will be laid down, by section:

* **Server access** and the **Member** role
* **Verification** (captcha)
* **Security** (including the honeypot channel)
* **Welcome**
* **Staff**
* **Tickets**
* **Text channels**
* **Bots**
* **Voice channels**
* **Roles**

Untick what you do not need: the **Preview** on the right follows live and shows the server as your members will see it. The **Select all**, **Select none** and **Back to the default selection** buttons speed up the choice.

Some items are locked: they go together with their section and untick with it.

Each channel states its audience:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open to everyone** | Visible even without any role. |
| **Closed: Member role only** | Accessible once the Member role is obtained. |
| **Closed: Staff role only** | Reserved for the team. |
| **Closed: visible only to the Unverified role** | Disappears once the captcha is passed. |

---

## How the Member role is obtained

Every channel is closed to `@everyone` and reopened to the **Member** role. Two ways to get it:

* **On arrival**: every newcomer receives the role as soon as they join, through the Welcome module's autorole. Nothing to pass, nothing to create.
* **After a captcha**: the newcomer first gets an *Unverified* role and sees only the verification channel. The Member role is granted once the code is typed back.

:::caution[Without the Member role]
If you untick the Member role, every channel is created **open to `@everyone`** and there is no access left to grant.
:::

The captcha starts in image mode, and its log goes to the staff log channel. The voice channel is ready: you can switch to voice mode from the [Anti-raid](/en/dashboard/moderation/anti-raid/) page.

## Modules enabled

The **Modules enabled** block ticks the modules to bring into service for the whole server:

* **Tickets**, **Levels and XP**, **Economy and RPG**
* **AutoMod**, on the recommended preset: Kotbo will start moderating messages
* **Nickname moderation**
* **Channel health**, analysis only: no channel is created or archived without your agreement

Some modules are enabled automatically because you are creating the matching channel.

:::note[Unticking disables nothing]
An unticked module is left **as it is**, on or off. The setup never turns off a module already in service.
:::

---

## Running the setup

The **Create the server** button asks for confirmation, recalling how many items will be created and which modules will be enabled.

Afterwards, Kotbo reports how many items were **created** and how many were **reused** (already present). The ticket panel is published automatically.

If the operation is interrupted, run it again: nothing will be created twice.

---

## After the setup

The rules are not published straight away: write your articles on the [Server rules](/en/dashboard/communaute/reglement/) page, then publish them into the channel that was created.
