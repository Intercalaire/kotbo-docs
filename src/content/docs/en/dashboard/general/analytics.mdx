<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Analytics</strong> page measures what actually happens on your server: messages, voice, joins, retention, moderation and invites, over the period you choose.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **General → Analytics** section: [Open Analytics ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/analytics).
Collection is switched off from **Settings → Activity statistics**. Without it, nothing is recorded any more.
:::

---

## Choosing the period

The offered periods are **24 hours**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days**, **365 days**, or a **custom** range. The **Live Feed** shows the time of the last update.

---

## The six categories

| Category | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | A general view of the period. |
| **Engagement** | Messages, voice, members, network, commands. |
| **Community** | Pulse, channels, social, word frequency. |
| **Growth** | Retention, cohorts, churn, hourly heatmap, week comparison. |
| **Moderation** | Moderation activity and staff performance. |
| **Invites** | Analysis of the invite codes. |

---

## Engagement

* **Top chatters** and **Top voice users**, with the full ranking, a search by name and pagination.
* **Popular text channels** and **popular voice channels**, by message volume and by time spent.
* **Commands executed**: overall use of the bot's commands and a breakdown per command.
* **Network**: the graph of interactions between members.

### Members

* **Population flow**: joins and leaves, daily or cumulative, with per-day averages and the **net** balance.
* **Active members**: peak concurrent activity, all-time peak, average and minimum.
* **Members displaying the server tag**: how the number of members showing the official tag evolves.
* A button lets you **sync existing members** to fill in the history.

---

## Growth

* **Returning members**: members active this week after 30 days or more away.
* **Retention by source**: the share of members still present 90 days later, per invite.
* **Join cohorts**: for each join week, the percentage still present at D+1, D+7 and D+30.
* **DAU / WAU / MAU** and **stickiness** (DAU/MAU).
* **Week vs week**: comparison of two periods on messages, voice time, joins, leaves and sanctions, with the absolute change and an estimated retention.
* **Hourly heatmap**: messages by day and by hour.

---

## Invites

**Total codes**, **active codes**, **total uses** and **average per code**, with the list of **popular codes** and **inactive codes**. A shortcut opens the [Invites](/en/dashboard/moderation/invitations/) page to act on them.

---

## Exporting

The **Export** button offers three formats:

* **CSV export**
* **XLSX export**
* **Image export**, which generates an image of every chart visible on screen

:::caution[You need data]
An export over an empty period is refused with the message *No chart data to export*.
:::

---

## Switching collection off

The **Activity statistics** switch on the [Settings](/en/dashboard/configuration/parametres/) page stops all recording: messages, voice minutes, reactions, threads, joins and leaves, per-member statistics, word frequency and usage rankings.

The other modules (moderation, tickets, levels and so on) keep working normally. Only the bot's technical measurements (execution times and errors, with no identity at all) are kept.
