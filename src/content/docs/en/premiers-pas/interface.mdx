## The four areas to know

1. **The active server**, in the top bar. Click it to switch servers.
2. **The sidebar**, grouped by purpose: General, Moderation, Security, XP system, Economy & RPG, Community, Staff, Cross-server and Configuration.
3. **The main area**, which shows the page you picked and its actions.
4. **Your profile**, in the top right, to replay the tutorial, open your settings or sign out.

## The overview

The home page brings together everything your team needs at a glance: members, online activity, voice channel usage, growth and recent events.

The **Edit layout** button lets you move, hide or resize the blocks. Your layout is personal: it does not change what the rest of the staff sees.

:::caution[Unsaved changes]
On configuration pages, a bar appears at the bottom of the screen as soon as a setting changes. Click **Save** before moving to another page. Kotbo warns you if you try to leave with pending changes.
:::

## Quick navigation

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">G</span> | Switch server |
| <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">D</span> | Open the overview |
| <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">M</span> | Open members |
| <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">C</span> | Open settings |
| <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">Shift</span> + <span class="kbd">N</span> | Show every shortcut |

Shortcuts do not fire while you are typing in a field or a text area.

## Badges and page states

- **Beta**: the feature works but may still change. You can report a problem from the banner on the page.
- **WIP**: the feature is not available yet, or needs specific activation.
- **Module disabled**: the page can disappear from the navigation until the module is enabled.

Carry on with [enabling your modules](../modules/).
