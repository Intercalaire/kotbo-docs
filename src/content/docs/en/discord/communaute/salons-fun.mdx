<p class="doc-lead">On Discord, the <strong>Fun channels</strong> module polices the game channels on its own, so your members can have fun without adding work for the moderation team.</p>

:::tip[Dashboard configuration]
To assign the channels and reset scores, read the [Fun channels configuration guide](/en/dashboard/communaute/salons-fun).
:::

---

## 1. The counting channel

The goal is to count as high as possible together, without making a mistake.

### Posting rules
* **Numbers only**: every message sent in this channel must start with the next number in the sequence (for example if the last number was `14`, the next message must be `15`).
* **No self-replies**: a user cannot post two numbers in a row. They have to wait for another member to post the next number.

### How Kotbo reacts to a mistake
If a player posts the wrong number, plain text, or tries to double-post:
* Kotbo instantly deletes the offending message.
* The bot may add a warning reaction.
* The current score is either kept or reset depending on your settings (if reset, players have to start again from `1`).

## 2. One Word Story

The community collaborates on an endless story by writing one word at a time.

### Posting rules
* **One word per message**: messages containing spaces, complex punctuation or several words are not allowed.
* **No consecutive turns**: you cannot post two words in a row. Another player has to add a word before you can write again.

### How Kotbo reacts
* Any message with more than one word, or posted consecutively by the same author, is deleted automatically by the bot within a second.
* The story reads smoothly from top to bottom in the channel.

---

## 3. Mystery Number (guess the number)

Kotbo generates a secret number in the background, and players have to guess it by posting values in the channel.

### How a round plays out
1. Players post numbers in the channel (for example `42`).
2. Kotbo checks the guess and replies publicly:
   * **"Higher!"** (if the mystery number is greater).
   * **"Lower!"** (if the mystery number is smaller).
3. Players adjust their next guesses accordingly.
4. As soon as a member hits the exact number:
   * The bot announces the win and mentions the winner.
   * The round ends, and a new mystery number is generated automatically (or manually by staff from the dashboard).
