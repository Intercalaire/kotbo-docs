<p class="doc-lead">On Discord, the <strong>Tickets</strong> module gives members a smooth, secure way to reach the server's support team in private.</p>

:::tip[Dashboard configuration]
To configure the panels and ticket categories and to track satisfaction, read the [Tickets configuration guide](/en/dashboard/communaute/tickets).
:::

---

## How a member opens a ticket

### 1. The welcome panel
In the designated support channel (for example `#support-tickets`), members see an introduction message (an embed) containing:
* The title and instructions written by the administrator.
* **The opening selector**: either coloured **buttons** (for example `Report a bug`, `Shop`), or a **dropdown** listing every available support category.

### 2. The private space is created
As soon as the member clicks a button or picks an option:
* Kotbo creates the conversation space according to the active mode:
  * **A temporary text channel** (for example `#ticket-john-001`) placed in the configured ticket category.
  * **A private thread** created under the panel's channel.
  * **Direct messages (DM)**: the member talks privately with the bot, which relays their messages to the staff.
* Only the member who opened the ticket, administrators and holders of the configured **staff role** (for example `@Support Staff`) can access that new space.

---

## How support unfolds on Discord

Once the ticket is open, the member and the moderators can talk. Kotbo posts an introduction message in the channel with the quick actions:

### 1. Claiming the ticket
* Staff members can click the **Claim** button.
* Kotbo renames the channel or updates the permissions to signal that this moderator is personally handling the case.

### 2. Exchanging messages
* Staff members can reply directly on Discord.
* Their replies are also mirrored in real time in the Kotbo dashboard interface, so other moderators can follow along.

### 3. Closing the ticket
Once the request is resolved:
* The member or the staff clicks the **Close** button.
* The bot asks for a justification (a closing reason).
* The Discord channel is locked, archived or deleted after a short safety delay.

---

## End-of-ticket satisfaction survey

As soon as the ticket is closed:
* Kotbo sends the user a direct message (or a channel message before deletion).
* The message invites them to give a rating from **1 to 5 stars** by clicking interactive smiley buttons.
* A form opens so they can write a comment about how they were treated or how their request was resolved.
* This feedback feeds directly into the charts in the dashboard's **Satisfaction** tab.
